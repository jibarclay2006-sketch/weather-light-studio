Using Weather Light Studio

  1. Choose a color, then click or drag on the 8 × 8 drawing grid. On a keyboard, use arrow keys to move and Enter to paint.
  2. Use Duplicate to keep a drawing and change a few pixels in the next frame. Trace last frame shows the previous frame behind unlit cells.
  3. Press Play and switch between the plain grid and the housing preview. Frame duration is how long each drawing stays on screen.
  4. Choose Export → Editable project to send your animation. The recipient uses Open to load it.
  5. To install an animation, export For the Weather Light. Unzip the animation-ready Arduino sketch and put CustomAnimation.h next to WeatherLight.ino. Follow the included guide to upload through the rear DATA port. Tap Mode until the OLED shows CUSTOM.

Shortcuts on desktop include B for Draw, E for Eraser, F for Fill, I for Pick color, and Space to play from the workspace.
